A trust dedicated to classical music

Dr. Ishwar Koujalgi Memorial Charitable Trust was established in Bidar to honour the memory of Dr. Ishwar Koujalgi through the classical arts he loved. Each year, under the banner of Sangeet Shraavana, the trust brings artists to the city and felicitates those who keep these traditions alive.

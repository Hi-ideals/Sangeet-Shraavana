Our story

A charitable tribute to Dr. Ishwar Koujalgi, offered through music, since 2013.

Where it began

Dr. Ishwar Koujalgi Memorial Charitable Trust was established in Bidar in 2013, founded in memory of Dr. Ishwar Koujalgi. The first Sangeet Shraavana program was a single evening recital — a way of offering something he loved back to the community.

Over twelve years, that one evening grew into an annual program spanning six art forms, regular felicitations of classical artists, and recitals open to anyone in Bidar who wishes to attend. The trust has never charged for entry — the tribute was always meant to be shared.

What we believe

Three ideas guide every program the trust holds.

A tribute, not a transaction

Every program is offered in memory of Dr. Ishwar Koujalgi. Entry is free — the trust exists to give something back, not to run a business.

Open to everyone

No program requires a ticket, a membership, or prior knowledge of classical music. Anyone in Bidar is welcome to attend.

Artists, properly honoured

Felicitating accomplished and overlooked artists is as central to our work as the recitals themselves.
